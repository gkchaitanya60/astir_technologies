Turn email bounces into actionable intelligence.

Created through a joint venture between Astir IT Solutions and Nurture Marketing, BounceBack AI identifies the individuals behind hard-bounced emails and tracks their career transitions.

Teams can discover where contacts have moved, understand changes in responsibility and reconnect when a former contact becomes a potential buyer in a new organization.

Built around the revenue workflow.

  • Identify people behind hard-bounced email addresses
  • Track job changes, promotions and company moves
  • Recognize transitions into decision-making roles
  • Re-engage contacts at relevant career moments
